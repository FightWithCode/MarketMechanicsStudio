# The Anatomy of a Short Squeeze set art

The four background plates were created with the built-in ImageGen tool. They are stored in [the film's set directory](../../public/films/short-squeeze). The actor poses, prop movements, camera, and cuts are drawn from code.

**Apartment**

> Use case: illustration-story. Create a single 3:2 landscape background plate for an original hand-inked 2D financial thriller short. Quiet cramped New York apartment office at late night, viewed from opposite the desk as a stage set. Sophisticated animated film production background. Stylized hand-painted watercolor gouache, charcoal blue ink contours on architecture, subtle paper tooth, worn wood grain, believable perspective, gritty rich dark indigo/mauve shadows with pockets of warm light. At the top left a black sash window shows rainy violet-blue skyline. On the far right an old olive banker desk lamp with a curved metal shade and tiny framed picture. Center rear has a worn burgundy swivel chair facing the camera, empty. Across the lower quarter a broad dark walnut writing desk with a thin front lip and clear central desktop. Camera looking mostly straight at the back wall, enough desk surface to see a phone on it later. The middle of the frame directly above the desk stays free of objects to composite an animated character in that area later. Central 50% includes the chair and light from the window so a vertical center crop works. Environmental details in side wings, weathered plaster, coffee rings, abstract rain reflections. Full bleed film still, no borders, no split screen. No people, no character, no hands, no readable text, no stock charts, no phone, no captions, no watermark.

**Broker's counter**

> Use case: illustration-story. One full-bleed landscape 3:2 hand painted animation background for an original finance thriller. A small old New York stock transfer office, imagined as a late 1970s service counter. Front-on two-character stage layout, clear central transaction space. An arched brass-framed service opening and cream plaster wall, olive-tiled wainscoting, a few ledger shelves and a dark green metal vault door at the very far left. The counter is a long very low dark reddish wood ledge along the bottom 22 percent, with clear flat top, no glass between the camera and the figures to be composited. Tall pendant lamp casts a shaft of soft apricot light into the central third of the film image. A cool patch of teal on the rear wall. Emphasize subtly crooked hand inked outlines, natural aged materials, old paper texture, shadow falloff, detail at the edges. Rich contrast and an old cel animation look. The center two thirds has a clean value structure and uninterrupted negative space above the counter for hand-drawn people to act in. The empty stage set feels cinematic, not infographic. No people, no lettering, no signage, no hands, no documents on the counter, no frame or watermark.

**Exchange exterior**

> Use case: illustration-story. Single full bleed landscape 3:2 hand-painted 2D animated film environment with black-blue ink linework and painterly gouache: one rain slick narrow financial district street in Manhattan at night. A fictional stock exchange shop with distinctive mustard-yellow awning and aged, terracotta-colored brickwork is the center of the image, the old bronze arched front door around 50% x, 54% y, with warm amber light pouring from the windows. Buildings rise to upper corners, bluish dusty midnight sky, a few office windows lit. Oblique street perspective, street-level camera, light reflects in wet asphalt and puddles, a manhole cover and loose scraps of paper, gritty dark plum shadows, full convincing scenery. The LOWER HALF has a broad walkable wet sidewalk and a strip of cobblestone street for animated characters to be added. Composition works cropped into vertical through the middle door. Warm signage is a blank face, no letters, no labels. No people, no vehicles, no graphic elements, no captions, no watermark. Original moody animation studio background, real location depth and soft fog.

**Auction hall**

> Use case: illustration-story. One cinematic landscape 3:2 painted animation background: the interior of a retro stock exchange auction hall, designed as an original hand-inked thriller set, camera looking down from a shallow elevated position at a long ochre wooden auction counter, high industrial skylight and gridded wall of amber electronic price-board lamps (all blank). Deep blue-gray architecture, emerald rails, amber spotlights, terracotta cable casing. Spacious layered stepped theater-like floor, scattered pieces of blank paper, moving picture camera depth lines to a small booth at center rear, tall dark walls, colored illumination and subtle grain. A diagonal aisle is visibly clear from bottom left to center counter. The entire center half is open for characters to be added. The bottom third is a walkable floor with patterned stone tile. Lived-in animated backgrounds, dense detail in the wings, precise loose ink outlines and visible gouache grain. No characters or crowd, no words or numbers, no charts, no captions, no watermark, no separate panels.
